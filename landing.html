ENEMY CLASSIFICATION

THE AUTOMATA LEGION

6 distinct biomechanical combat chassis deployed by the rogue Clockwork Empire to breach your Sanctum.

Skitter Scout

Ground · Light Chassis · 2.7 Speed

Rapid multi-legged reconnaissance arachnid. Deploys in high-density swarm formations to overwhelm single-target batteries.

Tread Tank

Ground · Heavy Armored (8) · 1.35 Speed

Continuous-track iron siege tortoise. Highly resilient against kinetic rounds; requires energy beams to pierce its outer shell.

Rotor Drone

Air Skirmisher · Flying · 2.2 Speed

Gyroscopic flyer that ignores labyrinth walls, soaring straight from spawn to Sanctum. High priority for flak mortars.

Diesel Walker

Ground · Armored Biped (10) · 0.95 Speed

Imposing bipedal mecha with high hit-point reserves. Stepping steadily through maze turns while absorbing immense firepower.

Zeppelin Sky-Hull

Air Dreadnought · Heavy Armored (6) · 0.8 Speed

Massive aerial leviathan carrying extensive durability. Leaking this titan will deal 6 lethal wounds to the Sanctum.

Goliath Colossus

Raid Boss · Maximum Armor (16) · 0.65 Speed

Catastrophic boss automata appearing on decennial waves. With 4,400+ HP and 10 leak penalty, failure to contain means defeat.

THE CHRONICLES OF OLYMPOS

SACRED FLORA & CLOCKWORK RUINS

Suspended high above the bronze-age cloudline drifts the Floating Sanctum of Olympos. Within its temple peristyles, radiant clockwork flora—golden chrysanthemums, blooming lotus chronometers, and ancient olive spires—pulse with harmonic solar energy.

When the Automata Empire shattered the peace to harvest the Sanctum's infinite core, the temple priests forged the Aegis Defense Grid: converting living vegetation and marble stonework into an impenetrable tactical labyrinth.
